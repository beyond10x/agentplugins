`docs/tutorials/first-governed-plan-2026-09-28.md` were in no sidebar, so Docusaurus rendered them
without one. A reader who followed the "2026-09-28 recording" link from a tutorial landed on a page
with no navigation but the navbar.

being listed in it. The UXF-003 entry of the adapter's `KNOWN_PROBLEMS` is gone; the gate now fails
when a doc page has no sidebar at a gating size.
