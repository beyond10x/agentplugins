`docs/tutorials/first-governed-plan-2026-09-28.md` are in no sidebar, so Docusaurus renders them
without one. A reader who follows the "2026-09-28 recording" link from a tutorial lands on a page
with no navigation but the navbar.

Fix (prose owner, `website/docs`): front matter `displayed_sidebar: docs` in both files. The page
then shows the docs sidebar without being listed in it. The change removes the UXF-003 entry from
`KNOWN_PROBLEMS`.
